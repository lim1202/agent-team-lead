# Task record

Optional outline for projects without an existing task record. Adapt it to the project; omit irrelevant sections and reference existing records. Keep local paths, session identifiers, raw logs, credentials, and private authorization evidence in an appropriate untracked companion record. Do not commit this outline with sensitive values filled in to a public repository.

## Outcome and kickoff

Record the requested outcome, agreed scope/exclusions, acceptance approach, executor selection and reason, estimated delivery time and uncertainty, kickoff confirmation, and the source and boundary of authority. Link the source of approval; do not infer permission from a checked box.

## Ownership and execution

Record the current coordinator, responsibilities, execution assignments, branch/base/candidate, and a reference to the local execution-identity record. On takeover, record who relinquished coordination, who accepted it, and the notification target change or manual retrieval fallback.

## Current checkpoint

Record when state was last checked, completed work, observed activity, blockers and their owners, work that continues, next action, and any changed delivery expectation. Separate observation from interpretation.

## Verification and acceptance

Link original test/review results and the revision/content and environment they cover. Record outstanding findings, partial or unexecuted checks, coordinator verification, and acceptance with limitations. Do not substitute a pass label for evidence.

## Decisions and uncertain actions

Keep consequential decisions and remaining authority questions. For an external operation whose result is unknown, record its intent, evidence, and the reconciliation needed before retrying; do not paste secret-bearing commands.

## Closeout

Record deliverable entry points, actual elapsed time versus estimate, known usage-limit effects, authorized integration, resources cleaned or intentionally retained, and remaining issues. Preserve this record after temporary worktrees and monitors are removed.
